# Majestic Theatre — V1.0 Reconstruction Brief

## Purpose

Create an evidence-bounded, mobile-first architectural study of the Majestic Theatre's opera-house era for review in a browser before mobile integration.

## Scope

- Exterior facade and shallow side mass only.
- Target period: **1927 opera-house origin**, with “completed circa 1928” retained in the evidence notes.
- Camera: a **180-degree facade orbit**, from far-left to far-right, with no claim about unseen rear geometry.
- Visual language: matte white massing, black contour lines, light beige shadow, transparent or neutral background.
- Three hotspots: opera-character tiles, flying dragons, and the Art Deco/Chinese architectural combination.

## Exclusions

- No interior, stage, auditorium or original floor plan.
- No full 360-degree orbit, aerial flythrough, AR, VR, real-time model viewer or photorealistic texture work.
- No archival image embedded in the prototype unless separately cleared for reuse.

## Approved visual-direction study

`prototype/majestic-v1/assets/majestic-style-study-v1.png` is the founder-approved aesthetic target for the Majestic Theatre 3D component: a white architectural study with strong dark contouring, restrained shadow and selective opera/dragon facade expression. It remains an interpretive AI-generated study, not historical evidence or a historic rendering. The final model must be checked feature-by-feature against the evidence register and retain the required reconstruction disclosure.

## Required disclosure

> Visual reconstruction based on historical references. The model focuses on documented facade features; it does not claim a complete survey of the original building.

## Content boundaries

- Say **transformed**, **repurposed**, or **changed use**; do not say demolished.
- Use the approved date wording rather than asserting one uncontested completion date.
- Keep the exterior scene intentionally schematic until the evidence register supports more detail.
