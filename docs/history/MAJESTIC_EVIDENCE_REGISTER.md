# Majestic Theatre — Evidence Register

**Purpose:** constrain the v1.0 browser prototype and later mobile asset package. This register is historical evidence, not a licence to reproduce its source images.

## Approved historical framing

- **Name:** The Majestic Theatre; originally the Tien Yien Moh Toi / Theatre of Heavenly Shows.
- **Location:** 80 Eu Tong Sen Street, Chinatown.
- **Origin:** commissioned by Eu Tong Sen as a Cantonese opera house for his wife.
- **Date wording:** “commissioned/built in 1927; completed circa 1928.” URA and Roots use 1927; NLB and BiblioAsia records also use 1928 in some descriptions.
- **Architectural reading:** Art Deco massing with Chinese-inspired decorative motifs; designed by Swan & Maclaren according to NLB/BiblioAsia records.
- **Transformation:** opera house, later cinema, then a shopping-mall conversion in 2003. The building survives and is conserved.

## Rights and asset rules

- NLB lists the 1950 exterior photograph as a digital copy copyright National Library Board Singapore 2007, with the original attributed to Peh Heng Huat. Treat it as reference-only unless specific reuse permission is obtained.
- The v1.0 browser prototype must use original schematic geometry and original rendering. It must not embed, trace verbatim, or redistribute archival photographs without clearance.
- Every final screen must include the reconstruction disclosure and source link required by the PRD.

## V1.0 hotspot brief

1. **Stories in the tiles** — hand-painted tiles depicting opera characters.
2. **Dragons on the facade** — flying-dragon decoration identified in the URA record.
3. **Art Deco, Chinese voice** — the building’s Art Deco massing combined with Chinese-inspired motifs.
