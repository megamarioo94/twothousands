About HUEstory.

Colour is a prompt for exploration, not evidence that the surface being scanned is connected to a historical place, map or object.

Stories are locally bundled. No camera images, scanned colours, device location, identifiers or session progress are uploaded or retained. Session progress is only held in memory.

Illustrated viewer graphics are original explanatory placeholders. They are not reproductions of archival maps, measured reconstructions, or faithful 3D scans. Replace these only after asset permissions and provenance are confirmed.
